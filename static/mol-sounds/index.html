Can you hear a force field fail?

A molecular dynamics trajectory turned into a chord: one voice per bond type, pitch following how far the bonds stretch. One run is stable; in the other, the machine-learned force field slowly loses the molecule. The question for this pilot: does a listener notice before the automated checks do?

3 · How it works

Sound

Bonds are found in the first frame of the stable run. Each frame, every bond's deviation from its mean length in the stable run is taken, bonds are grouped by element pair (C–H, C–O, O–H, C–C), and each group becomes one voice whose feature ri(t) is the largest deviation in the group. The voice sounds at

fi(t) = f0,i · 2c (ri(t) − r̄i) / σi

with r̄i, σi the feature's mean and spread in the stable run, base notes 220, 275, 330 and 440 Hz (a just A-major chord), and gain c = 0.5 octave per standard deviation. Sines with continuous phase, summed and soft-limited; 25 MD frames per second of audio. The optional loudness channel scales with the largest atomic force.

Automated checks

A frame is flagged when the total energy has drifted more than 11.5 kcal/mol (0.5 eV) from the start, the largest atomic force exceeds 115 kcal/mol/Å (5 eV/Å), or two atoms come closer than 0.75 Å. Both stable runs never trip a check; the failing run is first flagged at frame 333 (energy drift), then at 353 (force).

Data

Glyceraldehyde, C3H6O3, with one SchNetPack force field, NVE velocity Verlet at 0.5 fs, all runs from the same starting geometry: a 2 000-step run initialised at 300 K, a 20 000-step run at 300 K for extra stable clips, and a 2 000-step run initialised at 400 K that fails.

Caveats

  • The failing run starts hotter, so its voices wobble more from the first frame, before anything has gone wrong. A listener can pick the hot run without hearing the failure itself; a fair test needs a stable run at the same temperature.
  • All four failing clips come from one trajectory and three of them overlap, so a listener may recognise them.
  • One molecule, one force field, one listener at a time. Read the results as a pilot, not evidence.
